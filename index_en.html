FAQ

Q. Where is my data stored?
A. All data is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers.

Q. Do I need to create an account?
A. No account is required. The app is ready to use immediately after installation.

Q. Can I back up my data?
A. Device backups may restore your data, but this is not guaranteed. We recommend exporting your list as CSV as a manual backup. We cannot guarantee successful data migration.
